# Summary

Eight files in `requirements/sets/bg-national/`, a first and second exercise
per age group.[^bg-national-2026] They mirror the
[FIG age groups](fig.md) with lower caps at the younger ages.

| Age group | First exercise | Second exercise |
|---|---|---|
| 10 yrs | 10 different elements, at most 2 under ¾ somersault, special requirements: to front or back, from front or back. No difficulty. | Cap **1.3**. Qualify for British Championships: difficulty 4.7, total 71.0 |
| 11–12 | As 10 yrs plus no triples. No difficulty. | Cap **1.7**; no triples. Qualify: 6.3 / 73.0 |
| 13–14 & 15–16 | At most 1 under ¾ somersault; no quads. No difficulty. | Cap **2.1**; no quads. Qualify (men / women): 13–14 8.3 / 76.0 and 7.8 / 75.0; 15–16 10.0 / 81.0 and 9.0 / 80.0 |
| 17–21 | Every element at least ¾ somersault; no quads. **Two elements score difficulty.** | Cap **2.2**; no quads. Qualify: men 11.0 / 86.0, women 10.0 / 85.0 |

The qualifying scores are in the descriptions only and are not checked. Missing
them carries no penalty.
