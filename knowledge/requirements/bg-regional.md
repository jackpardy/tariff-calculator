| Regional L4 | Voluntary: 10 different elements; at most 3 (10 yrs, 11–12) or 2 (13+) under ¾ somersault; no triples. Special requirements: landing on the back (10 yrs); also landing on the front (11–12); also a full somersault with at least a full twist (13+) | Minimum 4.2–5.0 and cap 0.8–1.5 by age (10, 11–12, 13–14, 15–16, 17+); no triples |

Below the minimum difficulty a gymnast can't qualify for the Regional or
Inter-Regional Final.

Club L1, for example, is: front landing, to feet, straddle jump, seat landing,
to feet, ½ twist jump, tuck jump, pike jump, back landing, to feet.
