# Features

* [Routine builder](routine-builder.md) - The calculator page, where users add skills through the picker, search or builder, keep several routines on the phone, check one against requirements, choose its checks, and show a second routine beside it.
* [Sharing by link and QR code](sharing.md) - Routines and requirements are shared as a compressed link fragment that never reaches the server, with a QR code for sharing in person.
* [View screen](view.md) - A full-screen, scroll-free view of a routine, or a level's pair of routines, with a Show menu to turn each kind of information on or off.
* [Tariff sheet](tariff-sheet.md) - A printable competition card for the current routine, showing elements in order with FIG notation, difficulty, required-element ticks and a judge column, plus optional detail fields.
* [Compare page](compare.md) - Shows two saved routines side by side, read-only, with their validation and totals.
* [Competition entries](competition-entries.md) - What organisers, club competition secretaries, members and coaches see when entries are collected for a competition. From entering to the day itself: entries with their problems, cards, video proof, coach sign-off and approved coaches, fees, limits and waiting lists, late changes, officials, the timetable and its rota, helpers' links, notifications, one link for a person's several roles, all people, and on the day check-in, who's up, desk messages and the venue screen. Live at `/competitions`.
* [Sandbox](sandbox.md) - A second instance at sandbox.tariff.pardy.ie, full of made-up ISTO clubs and gymnasts in two competitions (entries open; on the day, its morning under way), with a "Try it as…" start page, notifications off, a banner on every page, and the maintainer's buttons to reset it and restart its day.
