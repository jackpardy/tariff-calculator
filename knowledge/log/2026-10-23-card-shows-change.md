* **Update**: [A card being changed shows the change on its own head](features/routine-builder.md#changing-a-skill-in-its-card). While a skill is changed in its card, the card's head shows it as it will be, live (number, name or label, notation, tariff), tinted and marked "not saved", with **Update** and **Cancel** there in place of ↑, ↓, Edit and ✕; Cancel or Escape puts it back. Under the head come the shapes and **Add a label**, then **Change to another skill** (a result goes straight on the head), then **Build or adjust this skill**; the separate skill card shows only while that's open, as its readout. Enter, Enter, Enter still finds, puts on the card and updates, the head staying where it is. Side by side, in Levels mode and on a phone; adding is as it was.
