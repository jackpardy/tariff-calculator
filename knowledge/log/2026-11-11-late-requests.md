* **Update**: [Late requests](features/competition-entries.md#late-changes): once new entries close, members, comp secs and gymnasts can ask to enter late (allowed by default, with its own fee; one tap from an entry never sent); every request reaches the organiser at once with its coach's sign-off, the old sign-off setting now holds back Accept until a coach signs, and the organiser can reply to a request without deciding it, the asker answering on their page. Clubs choose, in four separate settings all off by default, whether members send their own entries, changes, late changes and requests to enter late (otherwise the comp sec sends them), and an organiser can take only what clubs send; a turned-down late entry can be asked for again.
