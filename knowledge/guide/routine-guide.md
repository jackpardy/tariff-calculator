Open <https://tariff.pardy.ie> on your phone or computer. There's nothing to
install and no account. Everything you make in the Routine Builder is saved in
that browser on that device. For an overview of what the app can do, see
[features](features.md). Running or entering a competition is in the
[competitions guide](competition-guide.md). This guide is also a
[PDF](routine-guide.pdf).

# 1. Add a skill

The **Add a skill** box is at the top.

1. **Find the skill**, in one of three ways:
   - **Tap it** in the picker. The tabs are Jumps, Body landings, Singles,
     Doubles and Triples. **Body landings** are the seat, back and front drops
     and their twists. **Singles** are every single somersault, twisting or
     not, including those landing on the body (Crash Dive, Back s/s To Seat,
     Ball-Out).
   - **Search** in the box above the picker by name ("barani", "back s/s",
     "full in rudy out") or by FIG notation ("4 1 o", "8 2 3 /"). Notation
     doesn't say forward or backward, so the results show both directions to
     choose from.
   - **Build it**: open **Build or adjust this skill** and set the somersault
     (in quarters: 4 is one somersault), the half twists (one box for each
     somersault in a double or triple), the direction, what it takes off from,
     and **Lands on seat** if it does. Use this for anything that isn't listed,
     or to adjust a skill you picked.
2. The card shows the skill's **name, FIG notation, take-off → landing and
   tariff**. If it says "This skill can't land like that", check the direction,
   somersault and twist.
3. **Choose the shape** (Tuck, Pike, Straight) if the card offers it. Each
   shape shows how much it changes the tariff. If no shapes are offered, the
   shape doesn't change the skill.
4. Optional: open **Add a label** to give it your own name. The official name
   stays alongside.
5. Tap **Add to routine**. If you have more than one routine, **Add to** picks
   which one gets the skill.

# 2. Build a routine

Your routine is in the **Routine Builder**, below the skill box. Make sure
**Routines** is selected at the top of it.

- **Reorder** skills by dragging a card, or with **↑** and **↓**.
- **Change a skill**: tap **Edit** on its card. It opens in the skill box, which
  now says "Edit skill 3". Change it, then tap **Update** (or **Cancel**).
- **Remove a skill**: tap **Del**.
- **See the details**: tap a card's name to open it.
- **How it's judged**: in an open card, this lists what the execution judges
  can take off for that skill, with the amounts and what each means: the
  shape, legs and feet, arms, opening and twist, and on the last skill the
  landing. It's from the FIG Code of Points 2025–2028 and works for any skill,
  including ones you build. The **Add a skill** box has the same button, to
  look a skill up before you add it.
- **Total Tariff** is under the cards. It counts only what the judges count: the
  first ten skills, a repeated skill once, and nothing after an interruption.
  When something doesn't count, **Raw Total** shows what everything adds up
  to.
- **Warnings** show on the card (⚠️) and under the total. They cover repeated
  skills, a take-off that doesn't match the last landing ("Bad Transition"), a
  straight jump or impossible landing that interrupts the routine, a tenth skill
  that doesn't land on feet, and more than ten skills.

**More than one routine.** The menu at the top lists your routines with their
number of skills.
- **+ New** starts another.
- **More** lets you **Rename**, **Duplicate** (good for trying out a change),
  **Save as a set routine** (see [section 5](#make-your-own-set-routine)),
  **Expand all** or **Collapse all** the skill cards, **Clear skills** or
  **Delete routine**.
- **Side by side with** shows a second routine beside this one. You can edit
  both, and skills that differ are shaded. On a phone they become two tabs.
  Use ⇄ to swap the columns and ✕ to stop.

# 3. Check a routine against a competition

1. Under **Check against**, choose the competition and level. The levels are
   grouped by organisation (BUCS, FIG age groups, British Gymnastics). Where a
   level's two voluntaries have different rules, they're listed separately,
   e.g. "BUCS L1 · first exercise" and "BUCS L1 · second exercise".
2. Each requirement is listed below the routine with ✓ or ✗, and the top shows
   how many are met (e.g. "4 of 5 met"). The skills that meet special
   requirements are marked.
3. **Checks** under the total let you change what is scored for this routine:
   - **Score difficulty**: untick it when difficulty isn't scored.
   - **Flag repeated elements**: untick it when repeats are allowed.
   - **Score … elements**: score only some elements, e.g. 2 in an AG3 or BG
     17–21 first exercise. The highest ones are picked for you. Tick
     **Scores** on the cards to pick them yourself, or tap
     **Choose automatically** to go back.

   The competition sets these for you. Change them only if your competition
   differs.

Built-in rules are drafted from each organisation's published rules. **Check
them against your competition's current rules.**

## Start from a set routine

**Start from a set...** (next to **+ New**) copies a set routine into your
routine: into the one on screen if it's empty, otherwise into a new routine
named after the set. Change it from there.

# 4. Work on a competition level

A level puts both exercises of a competition together, for example "set routine
option 1 or 2, then a voluntary".

1. At the top of the Routine Builder, choose **Levels**.
2. Pick your level from **+ New level...**, for example "BUCS L7".
3. The level's exercises are tabs, for example **Set 1 · Set 2 · Voluntary**.
   - A **set routine** tab shows the set exactly as prescribed. Opening it makes
     it your choice for that exercise, marked ●. **Start the voluntary from this
     set** copies it into your voluntary.
   - A **voluntary** tab needs a routine. Under **Routine**, choose **+ New
     routine**, one you've already built, or **Start from Set 1** (or 2). It's
     then checked against the voluntary's requirements, and you edit it like
     any other routine.
4. **Side by side with** shows another tab beside the open one, for example
   your set beside your voluntary.

Both exercises are checked together. Where the first exercise scores only some
elements (FIG AG3, BG National 17–21), those elements' difficulty carries over.
If you repeat one in the second exercise, it's flagged "Can't Repeat: Scored In
1st Exercise" and doesn't count.

You can keep several entries for the same level, say one per gymnast. Use
**More → Rename** to name them. Deleting a level keeps its routines. **Share**
(next to the level) sends the level with its voluntary routines, and it opens
in Levels mode for whoever receives it.

# 5. Write your own requirements (e.g. ISTO)

The app doesn't have every competition built in: ISTO's levels, for example,
aren't online yet. You can add them yourself.

1. Tap **Manage requirements** (under Check against). The requirements page
   opens in a new tab.
2. Under **Your requirements**, tap **+ New**. Or find a similar built-in one
   and tap **Duplicate** to start from it.
3. Give it a **Name**, and optionally a description and source (e.g. "ISTO
   handbook 2026–27").
4. **Add a rule** for each requirement. The kinds of rule are:
   - special requirements that must each be met by a different skill;
   - "one of these" (e.g. a ¾ somersault followed by a 1¼, *or* a full with a
     full twist);
   - how many skills of a kind (at least, at most, or none);
   - every skill of a kind;
   - no repeats;
   - number of skills;
   - difficulty range or cap;
   - a skill at a given position.

   Describe the skills by somersault (in quarters), twist (in half twists),
   direction, shape, take-off and landing, tariff, or exact FIG notation. Leave
   a field empty to allow anything. Add your own **Wording** so the rule reads
   the way your handbook says it.
5. Fix anything listed under **Fix before saving**, then tap **Save**. If the
   editor says **These can't all be met**, some rules contradict each other or
   ask for something no routine can do (say, at least 12 elements, or a double
   back that another rule bans). It catches the obvious cases, not every one.
   You can still save, but no routine will pass until you change them.

Your requirements now appear under **Check against → Your requirements**.

## Make your own set routine

A set routine is built like any routine, not written as rules:

1. Build it in the Routine Builder, skill by skill.
2. Choose **More → Save as a set routine** and give it a name. If the routine
   was already saved as one, you can update it or save a new one.

It then appears under **Start from a set... → Your set routines**, and on the
requirements page under **Your set routines** (**+ New set routine** there
makes one from a routine you've already built). To change it later, use **Edit
in the Routine Builder**.

## Make your own level

On the requirements page, under **Your levels**, tap **+ New**, or
**Duplicate** a built-in level. Choose what **The two exercises are**:

- **Set routine, then a voluntary** (e.g. BUCS L3–L7);
- **One voluntary**, with the same requirements for both exercises (e.g. BUCS
  FIG Level);
- **Two voluntaries**, each with its own requirements (e.g. FIG age groups);
- **Set routine for both exercises** (e.g. BG Club L1–L3).

Then choose the set routine(s) and requirements for each exercise. Add more
than one set routine when the gymnast chooses between them (option 1 or 2).
Your level then appears under **+ New level...** in Levels mode.

To pass them on, use **Share** to send a link, or **Export** to save a file that
someone else can **Import**.

# 6. View, print and share

- **View** (top of the Routine Builder) opens what's on screen full screen,
  sized to fit without scrolling: one routine, both routines when you have one
  side by side (each column also has its own **View** for just that routine),
  or in **Levels** mode the whole level: your set routine, your voluntary and
  the other set routine option. **Show** chooses what appears: skill names, FIG notation, difficulty,
  level and exercise, requirements met, warnings. **Share** sends what's on
  screen (the routine, both routines, or the level), as **Share** does in the
  Routine Builder. **Full screen** hides the
  browser bars (not on iPhone; add the page to your home screen instead).
- **Tariff Sheet** opens a printable competition card for the current routine.
  Fill in the details (gymnast, club, category, competition, round, coach). Use
  **Show on sheet** to hide any column or field you don't need, then tap
  **Print / Save as PDF**. Both stay at the top of the screen as you scroll.
  What you type on the sheet isn't saved.
- **Compare** shows two of your routines side by side, read-only.
- **Share** (next to + New) sends routines to someone else. Tick the ones to
  send, then use **Share…** for your phone's share menu, **Copy link**, or let
  them scan the QR code. Requirements a routine is checked against go with it.
  When they open the link, they choose what to add. Nothing is uploaded.

# Keeping your work safe

Everything is saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing the
browser's data, or using a private window, loses it. To keep a copy or move to
another phone, share your routines and requirements to yourself by link, or
export your requirements.

If a bar says **The app has been updated**, tap **Refresh**.
