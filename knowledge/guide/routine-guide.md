Open <https://tariff.pardy.ie> on your phone or computer. There's nothing to
install and no account. Everything you make in the Routine Builder is saved in
that browser on that device. For an overview of what the app can do, see
[features](features.md). Running or entering a competition is in the
[competitions guide](competition-guide.md). This guide is also a
[PDF](routine-guide.pdf).

# 1. Add a skill

The **Add a skill** box is at the top, with your routine under it.

1. **Find the skill**, in one of three ways:
   - **Tap it** in the picker. The tabs are Jumps, Body landings, Singles,
     Doubles and Triples. **Body landings** are the seat, back and front drops
     and their twists: first **From feet** (Seat Drop, Back Drop, Front Drop
     and their ½ twists), then **From a body landing**, in one group by where
     they land (To Feet, ½ Twist To Feet, To Seat…). Those take off from where
     the skill before them lands, so
     after a seat drop, **½ Twist To Seat** gives a swivel hips (Seat Half
     Twist To Seat), and after a back drop, **To Feet** gives Back To Feet.
     The skill card shows which one, its tariff, and why ("From seat, after
     Seat Drop · also called Swivel Hips"). The first skill takes off from
     feet. **Singles** are every single somersault, twisting or not,
     including those landing on the body (Crash Dive, Back s/s To Seat,
     Ball-Out).
   - **Search** in the box above the picker by name ("barani", "back s/s",
     "½ twist jump", "piked back", "full in rudy out", "swivel hips") or by
     FIG notation ("4 1 o", "8 2 3 /"). Type ½, 1/2 or half, and pike or
     piked, straight or layout, as you like. Notation doesn't say forward or
     backward, so the results show both directions to choose from, the one
     with a name first.
   - **Build it**: open **Build or adjust this skill** and set the somersault
     (in quarters: 4 is one somersault), the half twists (one box for each
     somersault in a double or triple), the direction, what it takes off from,
     and **Lands on seat** if it does. Use this for anything that isn't listed,
     or to adjust a skill you picked.
2. The card shows the skill's **name, FIG notation, take-off → landing and
   tariff**. If it says "This skill can't land like that", check the direction,
   somersault and twist.
3. **Choose the shape** (Tuck, Pike, Straight) if the card offers it. Each
   shape shows how much it changes the tariff. If no shapes are offered, the
   shape doesn't change the skill.
4. Optional: open **Add a label** to give it your own name. The official name
   stays alongside.
5. Tap **Add to routine**. If you have more than one routine, **Add to** picks
   which one gets the skill.

# 2. Build a routine

Your routine is under **Your routine**, below the skill box. Make sure **Routines** is selected at the top of it.

- **Reorder** skills by dragging a card, or with **↑** and **↓**.
- **Change a skill**: tap **Edit** on its card. It opens in the skill box, which
  now says "Edit skill 3". Change it, then tap **Update** (or **Cancel**).
- **See the details**: tap a card's name to open it.
- **Remove a skill**: tap the red **✕** beside Edit. Changed your mind? Tap
  **Undo** on the message that says so.
- **How it's judged**: in an open card, this lists what the execution judges
  can take off for that skill, with the amounts and what each means: the
  shape, legs and feet, arms, opening and twist, and on the last skill the
  landing. It's from the FIG Code of Points 2025–2028 and works for any skill,
  including ones you build. The **Add a skill** box has the same button, to
  look a skill up before you add it.
- **Total tariff** is under the cards. It counts only what the judges count: the
  first ten skills, a repeated skill once, and nothing after an interruption.
  When something doesn't count, the line under it says what everything adds up
  to ("Counting every element: 6.5").
- **Problems** are marked on the card (**!**) and listed under the total, in the
  same words as the tariff sheet, e.g. "Element 10 repeats an earlier element,
  so its difficulty doesn't count again." They cover repeated skills, a
  take-off that doesn't match the last landing, a straight jump or impossible
  landing that ends the routine, a tenth skill that doesn't land on feet, and
  more than ten skills. Straight jumps before the first other skill are
  preparation jumps: the routine starts after them, so they don't interrupt it.

**More than one routine.** The menu at the top lists your routines with their
number of skills ("Ciara voluntary · 10 skills").
- **+ New** starts another.
- **More** lets you **Rename**, **Duplicate** (good for trying out a change),
  **Save as a set routine** (see [section 5](#make-your-own-set-routine)),
  **Expand all** or **Collapse all** the skill cards, **Clear skills** or
  **Delete routine**.
- **Side by side with** shows a second routine beside this one. You can edit
  both. In the second routine, skills that differ from the first one's are
  shaded; untick **Shade skills that differ from the first** if you just want
  the two up together. On a phone they become two tabs.
  Use ⇄ to swap the columns and ✕ to stop.

# 3. Check a routine against a competition

1. Tap the button under **Check against** (it says **No requirements** at
   first) and choose the competition and level. Type to find one, e.g. "BUCS
   L7" or "novice", and press Enter for the first match. Your own requirements
   come first, then the ones you used lately, then each organisation (ISTO,
   BUCS, FIG age groups, British Gymnastics): tap one to open it. Where a
   level's two voluntaries have different rules, they're listed separately,
   e.g. "BUCS L1 · first exercise" and "BUCS L1 · second exercise".
2. How many are met shows straight under **Check against** (e.g. "4 of 5
   met"); tap **See which** for the list below the routine, each with ✓ or ✗. The skills that meet special
   requirements are marked.
3. **Checks** under the total let you change what is scored for this routine:
   - **Score difficulty**: untick it when difficulty isn't scored.
   - **Flag repeated elements**: untick it when repeats are allowed.
   - **Score … elements**: score only some elements, e.g. 2 in an AG3 or BG
     17–21 first exercise. The highest ones are picked for you. Tick
     **Scores** on the cards to pick them yourself, or tap
     **Choose automatically** to go back.

   The competition sets these for you. Change them only if your competition
   differs.

Built-in rules are drafted from each organisation's published rules (ISTO's
from its 2025 routines). **Check
them against your competition's current rules.**

## Start from a set routine

**Start from a set** (next to **+ New**) opens a list of set routines, yours
first, with a search like Check against's. It copies the one you choose into
your routine: into the one on screen if it's empty, otherwise into a new routine
named after the set. Change it from there.

# 4. Work on a competition level

A level puts both exercises of a competition together, for example "set routine
option 1 or 2, then a voluntary".

1. At the top of the Routine Builder, choose **Levels**.
2. Tap **+ New level** and pick your level, for example "BUCS L7". Type to
   find one, and press Enter for the first match.
3. The level's exercises are tabs, for example **Set 1 · Set 2 · Voluntary**.
   - A **set routine** tab shows the set exactly as prescribed. Opening it makes
     it your choice for that exercise, marked ●. **Start the voluntary from this
     set** copies it into your voluntary.
   - A **voluntary** tab needs a routine. Under **Routine**, choose **+ New
     routine**, one you've already built, or **Start from Set 1** (or 2). It's
     then checked against the voluntary's requirements, and you edit it like
     any other routine.
4. **Side by side with** shows another tab beside the open one, for example
   your set beside your voluntary.

Both exercises are checked together. Where the first exercise scores only some
elements (FIG AG3, BG National 17–21), those elements' difficulty carries over.
If you repeat one in the second exercise, it's flagged "Can't Repeat: Scored In
1st Exercise" and doesn't count.

You can keep several entries for the same level, say one per gymnast. Use
**More → Rename** to name them. Deleting a level keeps its routines. **Share**
(next to the level) sends the level with its voluntary routines, and it opens
in Levels mode for whoever receives it.

# 5. Write your own requirements

The app has ISTO's, BUCS's, FIG's and British Gymnastics' levels built in, but
not every competition's. You can add your own, or change a built-in one.

You write them beside your routine, which is checked against them as you go:

1. Under **Check against**, tap the button and then **+ Write your own
   requirements** at the bottom of the list. Or choose a built-in level and
   tap **Copy and change** to start from it, or choose one of yours and tap
   **Change**.
2. Give it a **Name**, and optionally a description and source (e.g. "Club
   championships 2027"). Leave **Elements may be repeated** unticked for the
   usual rule: a repeated element is flagged and scores no difficulty.
3. **Add a rule** for each requirement: Must include, Must not include, How
   many of a kind, This then this (skills in a row), Linked pairs of a kind,
   Number of skills, Difficulty between, A skill at a position,
   Every skill must be, or Special requirements (each met by a different
   skill). Pick the skill or kind of skill (any single somersault, a
   barani…) from the list, or describe it by somersault, twist, direction
   and shape. **+ Or another skill** adds another that counts too, so one
   rule can count, say, front somersaults or baranis. Add your own **Wording** so the rule reads the way your
   handbook says it.
4. Each rule says **Met** or **Not met** for the routine on screen, and the
   routine below is checked against them as they are.
5. Fix anything listed under **Fix before saving**, then tap **Save and
   check against these**. If the editor says **These can't all be met**,
   some rules contradict each other or ask for something no routine can do
   (say, at least 12 elements, or a double back that another rule bans). It
   catches the obvious cases, not every one.

**Manage requirements** (beside Check against), or **Requirements** in the
banner at the top, opens the requirements page, where you can do the same, and
also export, import, delete and make levels. Saving there with problems still
listed asks beside **Save**: **Save anyway** or **Keep editing**.

Your requirements now appear under **Check against → Yours**.

## Make your own set routine

A set routine is built like any routine, not written as rules. On the
requirements page, **+ New set routine** builds one right there: name it, find
each skill (by name or FIG notation), adjust it under **Build or adjust this
skill** and choose its shape on the card, then **Add to the set routine**;
move or remove skills in the list, then **Save set routine**. Or, in the
Routine Builder:

1. Build it in the Routine Builder, skill by skill.
2. Choose **More → Save as a set routine** and give it a name. If the routine
   was already saved as one, you can update it or save a new one.

It then appears under **Start from a set → Yours**, and on the
requirements page under **Your set routines** (**+ New set routine** there
makes one from a routine you've already built). To change it later, tap its
**Edit**, which opens it in the Routine Builder.

## Make your own level

On the requirements page, under **Your levels**, tap **+ New level**, or
**Duplicate** a built-in level. Choose what **The two exercises are**:

- **Set routine, then a voluntary** (e.g. BUCS L3–L7);
- **One voluntary**, with the same requirements for both exercises (e.g. BUCS
  FIG Level);
- **Two voluntaries**, each with its own requirements (e.g. FIG age groups);
- **Set routine for both exercises** (e.g. BG Club L1–L3).

Then choose the set routine(s) and requirements for each exercise. Add more
than one set routine when the gymnast chooses between them (option 1 or 2);
**+ Build a new set routine** builds one right there and chooses it.
For a voluntary, **+ Write new requirements** writes them right there, under
the level (**Copy and change** starts from the built-in ones chosen, **Change
these** changes your own); **Use in this level** saves them and chooses them.
They belong to the level, unless you tick **Also keep them in Your
requirements**, to use them elsewhere too.
Your level then appears under **+ New level** in Levels mode, under Yours.

Each of yours has the same buttons, in the same order: **Edit**, **Share**,
and **More** for the rest (**Duplicate**, **Export**, saving to a club or to
your page, and **Delete**, which asks on the row first: **Delete** or **Keep
it**). To pass them on, use **Share** to send a link, or **More → Export** to
save a file that someone else can **Import** (a level's file brings the
requirements it uses).

# 6. View, print and share

- **View** (beside **Your routine**) opens what's on screen full screen,
  sized to fit without scrolling: one routine, both routines when you have one
  side by side (each column also has its own **View** for just that routine),
  or in **Levels** mode the whole level: your set routine, your voluntary and
  the other set routine option. **Show** chooses what appears: skill names, FIG notation, difficulty,
  level and exercise, requirements met, warnings. **Share** sends what's on
  screen (the routine, both routines, or the level), as **Share** does in the
  Routine Builder. **Full screen** hides the
  browser bars (not on iPhone; add the page to your home screen instead).
- **Tariff sheet** opens a printable competition card for the current routine.
  Its **Check before printing** lists the routine's problems in the builder's
  words.
  Fill in the details (gymnast, club, category, competition, round, coach). Use
  **Show on sheet** to hide any column or field you don't need, then tap
  **Print / Save as PDF**. Both stay at the top of the screen as you scroll.
  What you type on the sheet isn't saved.
- **Compare** shows two of your routines side by side, read-only.
- **Share** (next to + New) sends routines to someone else. Tick the ones to
  send, then use **Share…** for your phone's share menu, **Copy link**, or let
  them scan the QR code. Requirements a routine is checked against go with it.
  When they open the link, they choose what to add. Nothing is uploaded.

# Keeping your work safe

Everything is saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing the
browser's data, or using a private window, loses it. To keep a copy or move to
another phone, share your routines and requirements to yourself by link, or
export your requirements. A club member can keep routines on their club
page (**More → Save to my page**), and members, coaches and gymnasts entering
on their own can keep requirements on their page (**Save to my page** on the
requirements page): they then open on any phone (see the [competitions
guide](competition-guide.md#keep-your-routines-on-your-page)).

If a bar says **The app has been updated**, tap **Refresh**.

Something confusing, wrong or missing? The **Feedback** tab at the right edge
of the screen sends it to the people who make the app.
