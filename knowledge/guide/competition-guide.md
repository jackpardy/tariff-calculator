This guide is also a [PDF](competition-guide.pdf). Routines are built and
checked in the Routine Builder: see the [routine builder guide](routine-guide.md).

Competitions collect every gymnast's card before the day, each checked against
its level, so the difficulty judges don't have to. They're at
<https://tariff.pardy.ie/competitions>. The calculator doesn't link there yet.

There are no accounts. Each person gets a **private link** instead: whoever has
it can use that page, so keep yours to yourself, and don't post anyone else's
in a group chat. Every link you get is saved in that browser, and
**tariff.pardy.ie/competitions** lists them all (competitions you run, clubs you
run, your clubs, clubs you coach, your own entries). On another phone, open the
link itself; copy it somewhere safe with **Copy** when you get it.

Who does what:

- The **organiser** creates the competition and sees every entry.
- A club's **competition secretary** (comp sec) creates the club, enters it in
  competitions and sends the members' entries.
- **Members** join their club and keep their own entries.
- **Coaches** sign off their members' routines, if the competition asks.
- A gymnast without a club can **enter on their own**, if the organiser allows.

A competition can offer several **disciplines**: individual trampoline,
**synchro**, **tumbling** and **DMT**, each at its own levels. A gymnast can
enter several. A synchro pair enters one routine, the level's voluntary,
checked like a trampoline routine; tumbling and DMT entries are a level only,
for now.

Routines come from the Routine Builder: build each voluntary there first, on
the same phone, then choose it when entering. A set routine needs nothing: it's
performed as written.

# 1. Run a competition (organisers)

1. Go to **tariff.pardy.ie/competitions** and tap **New competition**.
2. Fill in the **Name**, the **Date** and when **Entries close** (Irish and UK
   time). Tick the **Levels** gymnasts can enter; your own levels from the
   Routine Builder are under **Your own levels**.
3. Under **Synchro, tumbling and DMT**, tick any synchro levels, and type the
   tumbling and DMT levels, one a line, easiest first. Leave them empty for
   trampoline only. To run synchro levels together, pair them, one event a
   line: "BUCS L1 + BUCS L2" makes one event, "Synchro BUCS L1/L2", where each
   pair says which level they're doing.
4. Choose when **Entries open**: when you go live (the competition stays
   private while you set it up), at a set time, or now.
5. Choose whether **gymnasts can also enter on their own**, whether to ask for
   **video proof** (none, some skills such as any triple, or each whole
   routine), whether **entries need a coach's sign-off**, and whether to **rank
   men and women separately** (you can choose only some levels later).
6. Tap **Create competition**. You land on the entries page with your **admin
   link**. Save it: it's the only way back, and anyone who has it can manage
   the competition.
7. Open **Settings** (in the menu at the top) for the **club link** (give it to each club's
   comp sec) and the **link for entering on their own** (give it to gymnasts
   without a club). You can share them while the competition is private: they show its
   name, date and when entries open, but nobody can enter yet.
8. When you're ready, tap **Go live now**, or set a date and time for it to go
   live by itself. **Make private (pause entries)** under **Going live** on the
   **Settings** page stops entries again; the entries already made are kept.

**Finding your way round.** Every page of the organiser's section has a menu
under its title: **Entries**, **Timetable**, **On the day** (once the
timetable is published) and **Settings**. Tap a section to open it; its own
pages are listed under the bar, with the page you're on marked. **Entries** has
People, Late changes, Coaches (when you approve coaches) and Fees, with a count beside
Late changes and Coaches when something waits for you. **Timetable** has the
timetable, Officials, Simulate and the two "what if" pages. **On the day** has
the day's page, Desk messages and the Venue screen. **Settings** has the
Settings page and History. On a phone the bar scrolls sideways. A helper's link
shows only the sections it can open.

The entries page shows the counts, then a table for each event (a level, or
"Synchro BUCS L3", "Tumbling Novice"): gymnast (both names for a synchro pair), club,
each exercise, requirements met, problems, video and coach sign-off where
asked, when it was sent, and whether you've checked it. To find entries:
**search** for a gymnast (accents, case and spaces don't matter, so
"marykate" finds Mary Kate, and synchro partners count), filter by club, coach (or no coach), checked or not, signed off or
not, video, men or women, or **Problems only**, and sort by club, gymnast,
latest sent or coach. The filters stay in the page's address, so you can
bookmark or share a view; **Print cards** and **Download CSV** use them too. Each event's table starts folded (tap its heading to open it),
says how many of its entries are shown ("12 of 96"), and the list at the top
jumps to it; **Expand all** and **Collapse all** open or fold every one, and
your browser remembers which you left open. A search or filter opens the
events it finds people in. Tap a gymnast to see both exercises in full (a synchro
pair's one routine), checked as the Routine Builder checks them.

- **Mark checked** once you're happy, with a **note** for the club or gymnast
  if something needs fixing ("element 7 repeats element 3"). If they change
  the entry, it needs checking again.
- **Video**: open each video (it opens in a new tab), then **Video OK** or
  **Need more** with a note of what's missing.
- **Print cards** prints a competition card per exercise, filled in, for
  everyone shown, one level (**Print this level's cards**, inside the open
  level), the entries you haven't checked (**Print cards not checked**), or
  one gymnast (**Print card**). Save as PDF from the print dialog for one
  file.
- **Download CSV** gives every entry shown for a spreadsheet or scoring
  system.
- On the **Settings** page (a page of its own, with a list at the top to jump to
  each group) you can change when entries close or **Close
  entries now**, change the synchro, tumbling and DMT levels, put the levels
  in order (**Order of levels**, easiest first: built-in levels start in
  order, so check where your own levels sit), choose which
  levels rank men and women separately, turn entering on their own, video proof or
  coach sign-off on or off, **Replace the admin link** if it's been shared too
  widely, or delete the competition.
- **Links for your helpers** (under **Links** on the Settings page): name a link
  (who it's for) and choose what it can do: **Checking cards** (for
  difficulty judges: check cards, add notes, review videos), **Chairs of
  judges** (see the entries and print the chair of judges, score and
  marshal sheets), **Timetable and officials**, **Venue screen** (for a
  screen at the venue: what's on each area now and next, and nothing else; see
  [On the day](#on-the-day-venue-screen) below), or **Everything but links
  and deleting** (a co-organiser). Copy the link straight away: it's shown
  only once. **Remove** stops it working. **History** lists every change,
  when, and which link made it.
- **Changes and sign-off after entries close** (under **Entries and
  deadlines** on the Settings page): entries closing stops new entries; give **Changes to
  entries close** and **Coaches' sign-off closes** later dates to let clubs
  and gymnasts keep changing, withdrawing or sending again the entries
  they've made, and coaches keep signing off, until then.
- **Late changes:** tap **Late changes** to choose what clubs and gymnasts
  can ask for once entries close (a **level change**, a **routine change**,
  or both), each with a fee charged only if you accept. Each request shows
  whether it fits: the new card's problems, whether the new level is full,
  and where it would go in the draft timetable and what that moves.
  **Accept** applies it (it needs checking and signing off again, goes into
  the draft timetable for you to publish, and the fee goes on their
  invoice) or say why it's **Not accepted**. Members, comp secs and
  individuals ask with **Ask for a late change** on their pages. Where
  entries need sign-off, tick **A coach must sign off a late change before
  it comes to you**: the coach signs off the new card first (on their coach
  page, or an individual's coach through the sign-off link), and accepting
  carries that sign-off onto the entry.
- **Limits and waiting lists** (on the Settings page): give an
  event a limit and, once it's full, later entries go on its waiting list
  in the order they came in. The next one moves up by itself when an entry
  is withdrawn or removed, or you raise the limit; **Let in** takes someone
  in over the limit. Clubs and gymnasts see their place ("waiting list,
  3rd"); waiting entries aren't counted, charged, printed or timetabled.
- **Fees:** tap **Fees** to set what you charge (euro or sterling, an
  amount per entry for each discipline, a synchro pair counting as one,
  and a club fee if you have one) and how to pay. Each club and individual
  is listed with what's due, paid and left; record payments as they come
  (**Paid**, with the amount and a note), and print each one's **Invoice**.
  Clubs see what they owe on their club page, with their invoice;
  individuals on their entry. Removed, held and withdrawn entries aren't
  charged.
- **Concerns:** anyone with one of your links can **Flag a concern**, about
  an entry or generally ("element 7 isn't on the card"). They're listed at
  the top of your entries page, for you alone; mark each **Resolved**, with
  a note of what was done.
- **Approved coaches:** where entries need sign-off, tick **Coaches must be
  approved** and choose the lowest coaching level you accept in each
  discipline (Level 2 to start with; British Gymnastics and Gymnastics
  Ireland levels count alike, and a higher level counts). Clubs then send
  their coaches with their qualifications. **Coaches** (with how many are
  waiting) lists them by club: open each certificate, see whether they meet
  your level, and **Approve** or say **Not approved**, with a note the club
  sees. Coaches named by gymnasts entering on their own are listed under
  **Gymnasts on their own**. Find a coach by name, club or status (approved, waiting,
  not approved, withdrawn) with the search above the list. Only approved coaches can sign off, and only their
  sign-offs count:
  an entry signed off by anyone else says so. **Withdraw approval** stops a
  coach's sign-offs counting; approving them again, choose whether the
  sign-offs they gave before count again or only those from now on.

**Remove or hold entries**: tick entries in the tables (a table's header
ticks them all; filter by club first to take a whole club), then choose
**Remove** (they can't be sent or changed again until you restore them) or
**Hold, asking for changes** (the club or gymnast changes the entry and
sends it again, and you **Accept back in**), with an optional reason for
the club, the gymnast or both. They're no longer counted, printed or
timetabled, and are listed at the end under **Removed and on hold**, to
**Restore**.

An entry marked **Withdrawn** was withdrawn after the club sent it. It isn't
counted or printed, and goes when the club sends again. A competition and its
entries are deleted 120 days after the competition date.

## People

Tap **People** under **Entries** to see everyone at the competition in one
list, each person once: gymnasts (and synchro partners), officials and coaches.
A member who competes and judges is one row. The list shows their name, club
and what they do ("Trampoline BUCS L7, Synchro BUCS L6/L7 · judge, can chair ·
coaches 6"), with the number of people at the top. Type part of a name in
**Find a name**, or narrow the list by club or by what they do (gymnast,
official or coach). **Clear** shows everyone again.

Tap a name to see everything that person is doing, in one page for you to read
(they can't be changed from it): their entries and the status of each card, with
a link to the entry; what they offered to officiate, which events they may
judge, whether they're marked qualified and their seats on the panels; the
gymnasts they coach, each a link to that gymnast; and, once the timetable is
published, their flight, where they are in it, whether they're here, any
scratch warning and the desk messages sent to them. Names on the entries page,
the **Officials** page and the **Coaches** page open the same page (on the
entries page it's the small **person** link beside the name, and **partner** for
a synchro pair). A coach who is also a member shows as two people for now.
Helpers' links that can see the entries page can see People, but not the venue
screen link.

## Officials

Tap **Officials** on the entries page. It shows, for each discipline, how many
people can judge and chair against what one panel needs, and lists everyone:
gymnasts who offered (clubs send their members' offers with their entries),
and anyone you add.

- **Add someone** who isn't entering, such as a judge with no club: their name,
  club (if any), and what they can judge and help with.
- Tick **Qualified** for people qualified to judge.
- Under **Panels and who may judge**, each discipline's panel starts as the
  FIG Code of Points' (a Chair of Judges Panel, 6 execution and 2 difficulty
  judges) plus a recorder and a marshal; change any of it. Where there's no
  machine measuring horizontal displacement, give trampoline and synchro
  **HD** judges (2 for trampoline).
  Synchro has 2 **Sync** (synchronisation) judges to start with; set 0 if a
  machine measures synchronisation. A panel judges all of an event's
  flights, so every gymnast in it is scored by the same people; tick
  **Recorders can change** or **Marshals can change** to let those seats
  change between the event's flights.
  Choose who may
  judge: anyone up to the level they say, only levels below their own, or only
  people you mark qualified. "Up to" and "below" go by the **Order of levels**
  on the Settings page.
  To find people in a long list, type part of a name in **Find a name**, or
  narrow it by club, what they can do, the level they can judge (a level
  shows everyone who can judge it or a harder one) or qualified. **Clear**
  shows everyone again.

## Plan the timetable

Tap **Timetable** on the entries page. Set up the venue first; every number
has a starting value you can change.

1. **Days:** name each day and give its start and **end** time (when the venue
   must be empty of competition), and which areas are in use. **Add a day**
   for a competition over a weekend.
2. **Areas:** name each trampoline panel, tumbling track and DMT bed. Synchro
   runs on trampoline areas.
3. **Timings:** for each discipline, minutes per competitor (both rounds;
   5 is a starting point for trampoline, 2½ per synchro pair, who do one
   routine, and 2 for tumbling and DMT), minutes
   between flights (warm-up and changeover), and the largest flight. Set the
   **rest** a person gets between their events (from the end of one flight
   to the start of the next one's warm-up), and whether it's a must. Someone
   with little rest goes early in their first flight and late in the next. Where
   men and women are ranked separately, choose which levels also fly apart:
   a small level can fly mixed.
4. **Blocked time:** add lunch, awards or an ad hoc event, at a fixed time or
   anywhere between two times, on all areas or chosen ones. An ad hoc event
   can say what officials it needs (a chair, judges, a recorder, a marshal),
   and gets them from the rota. Each event is all before a break or all
   after it, unless you tick **Events can run across breaks**: then an event
   too long to fit between breaks (five flights, say) pauses for lunch and
   finishes afterwards.
5. **Rules:** put an event on an area or a day, before another event, or apart
   from another, as a **must** or a **prefer** (e.g. Elite synchro must be on
   Panel 1).
6. Tap **Plan**. Each event is split into even flights with running orders
   drawn at random (keeping a club's gymnasts apart where that's easy), and
   each event's flights are placed back to back on one area, so one panel
   can judge them all, with flight 1 first. No one is in two places at once,
   each day ends on time and the musts are kept, then as many preferences as
   fit. The report
   shows when each day's flights end, when the day finishes (awards and other
   blocked time included), the time free after the last flight for more
   flights, anything that doesn't fit, anyone with too
   little rest, names that look like one person entered twice, and changes
   that would make it all fit (including capping an event's entries, or
   letting events run across breaks).
   Planning tries to keep a coach's gymnasts off two areas at once; where it
   can't, **Coaches needed in two places** lists them, so you can move a
   flight, or the club can bring another coach.
   Someone who is a member and also a coach, or also enters on their own
   (one person, with one link), counts as one person: planning keeps their
   turns apart, tries not to put their gymnasts on while they compete, and
   the report says if they're coaching while competing, or short of rest.
   Planning also fills every panel from the people who've offered to judge
   and help (see **Officials**): never someone competing or on another panel
   at the time, and only in roles they may take. Each event's panel is
   filled once, with people free for all of its flights, and judges all of
   them. Clubs share judging their own gymnasts fairly.
7. Adjust manually: **Move event** moves all of an event's flights together
   to another day or area, **Earlier** swaps a flight with the one before it
   in its event, **Redraw order** draws a flight's running order again, and
   **Move** beside a gymnast puts them in another flight. To find someone
   on a long timetable, type part of their name in **Find a name**: only
   the flights with them show. **Jump to** at the top of the page goes
   straight to a day or an area. If an event's flights end up
   apart, or either side of a break you haven't allowed, it's listed as a
   problem. Entries made after planning are listed for you to place.
   Changing the setup asks you to **Plan** again. Open a flight's **Panel**
   to give a seat to someone else, for all the event's flights they had it
   for; **Assign officials again** redoes every panel and keeps the flights.
8. Under **Your rules**, **Officials: who does what** says who does what, as a
   must or a prefer: e.g. Mary **is** Chair of judges at Elite Women, Tom
   **doesn't officiate**, Ann **officiates only** on Saturday from 12:00. Then
   tap **Assign officials again**.
9. **Print marshal sheets** and **Print chair of judges sheets** (one area to a
   page, each flight with its panel), **Print score sheets** for the
   recorders (a flight to a landscape page: each gymnast in running order,
   a box for each judge's mark of each routine, difficulty, penalty and
   totals, the paper copy beside the scores entered online; officials can
   also open their own panels' sheets on their phone from **Score sheets
   for your panels**, beside their duties), and **Print the officials rota** (each person's duties) for the
   noticeboard. On a phone, swipe a sheet's table sideways to see all of
   it. **Panel timeline** shows every day
   side by side, a column per area, each flight and break with time running
   down evenly; **Timeline with officials** shows each area's day with a
   column for each seat on its panel and who has it. Print either (A3
   landscape) or **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet.
10. **Publish** to show clubs, members and gymnasts entering on their own their
   flight, area and warm-up time, and officials their duties. After that,
   anything you change (planning again, moving flights or gymnasts, giving
   seats to officials, keeping a "what if") is a **draft**: everyone else
   still sees what you published until you tap **Publish changes**. The page
   says when there are changes not published yet; **Discard changes** goes
   back to what's published, and **Unpublish** takes it down.
11. **On the day** (a button on the entries page once you've published; chairs
   of judges' and timetable links get it too): pick the day, then for each
   area tap **Started** when a flight begins its warm-up and **Finished**
   when it's done (**Undo** takes back the latest tap). On poor wifi a tap may
   be sent twice; the second changes nothing. Planned and actual
   times sit side by side, and each area says whether it's "Running 15 min
   late", "On time" or early. Where an area is late, **What if: see the rest
   of the day** opens the delay page filled in. Gymnasts and officials see "Panel 2 is
   running about 15 min late" on their page once an area is 5 minutes or more
   out. Under each flight, **Check-in** lists its gymnasts: tap **Here** as
   they arrive, or **Scratched** for a no-show (**Clear** takes it back).
   Scratched gymnasts are struck through on the printed sheets, and if one
   also has a seat on a panel that day, or is still entered in other flights
   that day, the page says so under their name, with a link to "what if they
   leave". Tap **Still officiating** or **Still competing** once you know
   they're staying and the warning goes. It also goes by itself if another of
   their entries is checked in, and the gymnast can clear it from their own
   page with **I'm here for the rest**. Under each flight, **Panel** does the
   same for the officials: tap **Here** as each arrives (it counts for the
   whole run of that event on the area, and clears a scratch warning), or
   **Missing**, which links to "what if they leave". Next to anyone who isn't
   here, **Notify** sends them, their club's comp sec and coaches, or both,
   a message saying where they should be. To find someone at the desk, type
   part of their name in **Find a name**: only the flights with them show,
   with their Check-in or Panel list open, and it stays that way as you tap
   Here. **Jump to** goes straight to an area.
   **Marshals: who's up now.** Once a flight is **Started**, its box shows who's
   first up. Tap **Next gymnast** as each gymnast starts their routine: the
   box shows who's on, their club and which routine it is (such as Set or
   Voluntary), and the venue screen shows them with the next two. Everyone
   does their 1st routine in running order, then everyone their 2nd, and
   scratched gymnasts are skipped. **Back** puts it right if you tapped too
   soon. After the last routine, tap **Finished** as usual: Next gymnast won't
   finish the flight for you.
   <a id="on-the-day-venue-screen"></a>**Venue screen** (in the menu, and a
   button at the top of the On the day page that opens it on the day you're
   looking at): what's on each area now and what's next, for a TV
   at the venue. On the **Settings** page make a **Venue screen** link, open
   it on the TV's browser and leave it: it keeps itself up to date, and that
   link can open nothing else. Each area has its own colour. Its card shows
   the flight on now and the next one, each with its gymnasts and their
   clubs, the warm-up time from the timetable, and how late the area is
   running. Each gymnast's running number (their place in the flight) is
   shown before their name, the same on every page and sheet, and it doesn't
   change when someone ahead of them is scratched. Once the marshal taps **Next gymnast**, the card shows who's on
   now, in large type, and the next two. Areas with nothing on soon shrink to a strip along the bottom.
   With two TVs, show some areas on each by adding them to the link, for
   example `…/screen?area=Panel 1&area=Panel 2`. If the TV loses its
   connection, it keeps showing the last update and says how old it is.
   To put a line across the top of every screen, use **On the venue
   screen** on the Desk messages page.
12. **Desk messages** (a button on the entries page, and a link at the top of
   On the day; chairs of judges' and timetable links get it too): send a
   message at once to a club, a flight (its gymnasts, its officials or both), all officials, one
   panel's officials, or people you pick (search by name or club to find them;
   people you've ticked stay ticked), and tick **Ask them to come to the
   organisers' desk** to call someone over. It is pushed and emailed straight
   away to those who asked to hear about changes, and shown in a "Messages
   from the organisers" box on each person's own page, so those who didn't
   still see it when they look. The page lists what you sent and how many
   were told.

**What if there's a delay?** (on the timetable page, once planned): choose the
day, when the hold-up starts, how many minutes and which areas (none ticked
for all). It shows which flights now start later or move to another area, when
each day's flights end, anything that no longer fits, and anyone needed in two
places. An event's flights stay together: if one would no longer finish
before a break, the rest of the event waits for it too (unless events can run
across breaks), and an event moves to another area with all its flights still
to come. Flights that have already run, or are under way, never move. Under **Ease it**, try what you'd do on the day: move, shorten or
work through lunch on those areas, fewer minutes between flights, quicker
turns, merging an event's flights into bigger ones, or letting the day run
over; it shows what that buys against the delay alone. Nothing is changed
unless you tap **Keep this in the draft timetable**; then publish the
changes when you're ready.

**What if an official has to leave?** (on the timetable page, once planned):
choose who, the day and time they go, and whether for good. Each of their
seats is filled for the rest of its event, by one person: someone free for
all of it, or by moving others round: a judge on the panel moving up to
chair, or someone moving across from another panel, their seat filled in
turn. Choose **easiest to fill** (the gap ends at a seat many
can take, such as recorder) or **fewest changes**. Nothing is changed unless
you tap **Keep this in the draft timetable**.

**Simulate** (on the timetable page) tries numbers before entries arrive, or
alongside them, without changing your timetable. It starts from your entries
and officials so far: change the entries per event, how many gymnasts there
are in all (fewer than the entries means some enter several disciplines), the
clubs, and how many people can judge, chair, record and marshal, then tap
**Simulate**. Under **Judges each club must bring**, say how many judges a club
brings for every so many of its competitors in a discipline (e.g. 1 per 8,
rounding up), and how many of them must be able to chair: the clubs' judges
then come from that, and the judges above are only your own. Each
discipline's judges are their own people, so a club needing 4 trampoline and
2 tumbling judges brings 6; say what percent of the clubs' judges can also
judge other disciplines as well. Each scenario shows when each day's flights
end, what doesn't fit and what would make it fit, how many judges the clubs
bring, and how many panel seats no one could take, side by side with the
others. **Change** starts a new one from its numbers; after changing the
venue setup, **Run again**.

# 2. Run your club (comp secs)

1. Go to **tariff.pardy.ie/competitions** and tap **New club**. Give its name
   and tap **Create club**. Save the **admin link** you're given.
2. Open **Members** and share the **Join link** in the club's chat. Members
   join with it.
3. To enter a competition, open the **club link** the organiser gave you,
   choose your club (or paste its admin link) and tap **Enter the club**.
   Members can then add their entries.

Your club page is in four sections, in a menu at the top: **Competitions**,
**Members**, **Coaches** and **Settings**. It opens on **Competitions**, which
lists each competition with every member's entry (one per discipline they
enter): its event, problems and status (**Not sent**, **Sent**, **Changed since
sent**, or withdrawn), plus any note from the organiser, and missing videos and
coach sign-off. Its problems are the ones the organiser sees: the card's, a
missing coach sign-off where the competition needs one, and a synchro pair at
the wrong level for their individual ones. Find a member with the search above
each competition's entries, or show only those with problems or still to send.
**Change**, under a member's name, changes their entry: that's part of
Competitions too. Your club's pages save their link in your browser each time
you open them, so **tariff.pardy.ie/competitions** lists the club.

- **Coaches for this competition** (where it approves coaches): tick the
  coaches who'll sign off its entries (your members' coaches start ticked)
  and tap **Send coaches**. Each shows whether the organiser has approved
  them, with their note. If you change a coach's qualifications, send them
  again for the organiser to approve again.
- **Send new and changed** sends the entries the competition doesn't have yet,
  or that changed since you sent them. **Send all again** sends everyone's and
  takes back withdrawn ones. The organiser only sees what you send, until
  entries close.
- **Change** edits a member's entry for them.
- **Members** (its own section): the **Join link**, then each member: choose
  their coach from the list, give a member who lost their link a **New link**
  (their old one stops working, and the new one opens only their pages at
  your club: they add their other links again), or **Remove** them. A member
  who also coaches needs no coach link: tap **Make a coach too** beside them,
  and their member link opens their coach's page as well. Making that coach a
  link of their own (**New link**) parts it from the member. Synchro entries that gymnasts from
  other clubs made with your members are listed here too. To find a member,
  search by name or narrow the list by coach, entries, or coach too. A member
  who keeps routines on their page shows how many: tap it to see them.
- **Coaches** (its own section; search by name, or narrow by who signs off
  and who has qualifications): type a name and tap **Add coach**, then send them the coach link
  shown (it's shown only then; **New link** makes another). Tick **Every coach
  sees every member** if your coaches share everyone. Untick a coach's
  **Signs off routines** if they shouldn't sign off: they still see their
  members' entries. Under **Add a qualification**, choose each coach's
  coaching qualification from the list and add a photo or PDF of its
  certificate (up to 3 MB, four per coach, and 60 MB for the whole club), for competitions that ask for
  qualified coaches. Only you, and organisers you send the coach to, can
  open it; it's kept until you remove it or the coach.
- **Settings** (its own section): replace the admin link, or delete the club. A
  club is deleted 13 months after anyone last used it (you, a coach or a
  member opening their page); Settings says when. **Opened from people's own
  links** lists anyone who added the admin link to their own page (see
  [One link for all your roles](#6-one-link-for-all-your-roles)), with
  **Remove**; replacing the admin link ends it for all of them.

# 3. Enter through your club (members)

1. Open your club's **Join link**, type your name and tap **Join**. Your own
   page opens; it's saved in this browser, so on this phone your entries are
   simply there.
2. Your page has three tabs. **Competitions** (the one it opens on) lists the
   competitions your club is in, each with its date and where your entries
   stand (for example **Not entered**, **Saved, not sent yet**, or **Timetable
   published**); tap one to open it. **Routines** has the routines you keep
   on your page (see [Keep your routines on your page](#keep-your-routines-on-your-page)).
   **Your details** has your personal link,
   to keep, **Your coach** to choose if your club has coaches, and **Your other
   links** (see [One link for all your roles](#6-one-link-for-all-your-roles)).
3. A competition's page has a row for each discipline it offers, closed until
   you tap it: the discipline, your level and its status (for example **Not
   entered**, **Checked**, **2 problems**, or **Waiting list, 3rd**). Open
   **Trampoline** (or Synchro, Tumbling, DMT), choose your **Level**, and Men
   or Women if asked. For trampoline, choose which set
   routine you're doing where there's a choice, and the routine for each
   voluntary from the routines kept on your page or saved in this browser;
   for synchro, just the
   pair's one routine, the level's voluntary. Add a **video link**
   if the competition asks (a YouTube video must be **unlisted, not private**;
   a Google Drive file shared with **anyone with the link**). Tap **Save**.
   Tumbling and DMT need only the level.
4. Your entry shows checked, with any problems; a row with problems to fix opens
   by itself when you save. Your comp sec sends it to the competition: the
   status says whether it's been sent, or changed since.

Under **Judging and helping**, say what you can do at the competition: judge a
discipline (up to a level, and whether you can chair), or help as a recorder
or marshal. Your comp sec can correct it, and sends it with the entries.

To change it, open the discipline's row, then **Change your entry**. To pull out of one discipline, tick
**Withdraw this entry** under it and tap **Withdraw**. Notes from the organiser
and your coach show on your entry, and once the organiser publishes the
timetable, your flight, area and warm-up time, and under **Officiating**
when and where you judge or help.

**My competition** (the one link on the competition's page) is the page for
the day: each of your events and its card's status, and once the timetable is
published, your events in the order you warm up, each with your flight, where
you are in the running order and about when each routine is, what you
officiate, and the whole timetable with you picked out. It has your tabs and a
link back to the competition's page. Once it's published, **Your timetable**
(on My competition) shows it laid out like the organiser's panel
timeline (days side by side, time running down evenly), with where you
compete and the panels you sit on in green and the rest faint (**Only
these** hides the rest); **Club timetable** does the same for everyone in
your club, naming who. The comp sec and coaches have **Club timetable** on
their pages too. Each prints on A3. **Add to calendar** (and **Club
calendar**) puts the same flights and duties in your phone's calendar; subscribe to it
and it updates when the timetable is published again.

**Tell me about changes** tells you when the organiser changes something
for you: when or where you compete, what you officiate, or your card
(checked, a note, removed or on hold). Tick what to hear about, then:

- **On this phone**: tap **Turn on notifications** and allow them. On an
  iPhone or iPad, first add the page to your home screen (Share, then **Add
  to Home Screen**), open it from there and turn them on.
- **By email** (where it's on): give your email, tick that you're 18 or over
  (or that it's a parent's email), then tap the link it sends to confirm.
  The email says only "a competition on tariff.pardy.ie"; the page it opens
  names the competition.

You get one message for each round of changes, a few minutes after the
organiser publishes, saying what was and what is now. The comp sec hears about all the club's members, and
a coach about the gymnasts they coach. Every email has a link to stop them:
it stops all the emails to that address about that competition.
To see it all, open your page from the link you saved: messages don't
carry your link, so they can't be used to open your page. Tapping one
opens **Competitions**, which lists the links saved on that phone.

## Keep your routines on your page

Routines you build in the Routine Builder are saved in that browser only. Keep
them on your club page too, and they open on any phone, your coach sees them
(and can leave you a note on each), and your comp sec can choose them if they
change an entry for you.

- In the **Routine Builder**, tap **More**, then **Save to my page**. (It's
  there once you've opened your club page on this phone.) Your page opens on
  **Routines**, with the routine kept. Saving it again from the same phone
  changes it there.
- Or open **Routines** on your page: under **Add routines from this browser**,
  tick the ones to keep and tap **Add to my page**.
- Tap a routine to see it checked, with any note from your coach. **Open in
  the Routine Builder** adds a copy to the builder on the phone you're using,
  to change and save again. **Rename** or **Delete from your page** (the
  builder's copy stays).

You can keep up to 30 routines.

## Synchro

Enter synchro like any discipline, and give your **partner's name** (and club,
if they have one): any gymnast, from your club, another, or none. A pair does
one routine: the level's voluntary. Your entry then shows a **partner link**:
send it to your partner. They open it and
confirm who they are with their own member page or entry link (or that they
have no other entry; if they've used the app on that phone before, they choose
themselves from a list), so the timetable never puts either of you in two places
at once. If you name a different partner, they confirm again. Once they've
confirmed, the entry shows on their own page under **Synchro as a partner**,
and to their coach and their club's comp sec, even if their club isn't
entered: checked, with its sign-off and, once the timetable is published,
the flight. You (or your comp sec) still make any changes. Both your pages
warn if the pair is at the wrong level for your individual levels.

A pair does the level they both compete at individually, or the easier of the
two if they're a level apart; more than a level apart, you usually can't pair.
If the organiser has paired synchro levels into one event (e.g. "BUCS
L1/L2"), choose the level you're doing, e.g. "BUCS L1/L2 (doing BUCS L2)".
The organiser sees a pair entered at another level flagged.

# 4. Sign off routines (coaches)

Your comp sec adds you and sends you your **coach link**. Your page lists each
competition with the entries you see: the members who chose you, those who
haven't chosen a coach, or everyone if the club says so, each with the
problems the organiser sees (a missing sign-off among them). If the club hasn't
set you to sign off routines, you see the entries but can't sign them off. A
competition that approves coaches says if you're waiting for its organiser
to approve you (or why they haven't): until they do, you can't sign off its
entries.

To find an entry, type part of a member's name in **Find a name**, or narrow
the list by competition, level, problems or sign-off.

1. Tap a member to see their entry, checked.
2. Tap **Sign off** if you've seen them perform it safely and it's ready, or
   **Not yet** with a note of what needs to change.

If they change the entry, it needs signing off again. Where a competition needs
sign-off, entries without it are flagged to the organiser.

Your page has two sections: **Entries** (the entries above) and **Gymnasts**:
the members you see, with how many routines each keeps on their page. Tap a
gymnast to see their routines, checked, and write **Your note** on any of
them for them to see, such as what to work on. Leave it empty to take it off.

# 5. Enter on your own

1. Open the competition's **link for entering on your own** from the organiser.
2. Choose the discipline at the top (where there's more than one), type your
   name, choose your **Level** (and Men or Women if asked), which set routine
   where there's a choice, and your voluntary from the routines saved in this
   browser, plus video links if asked. For synchro, give your partner's name.
   If you've entered on your own before, or you're a club member here, choose
   yourself under **Who's entering?** (or paste **your page's link**), so the
   entry joins your page; leave it blank the first time. Tap **Enter**.
3. You get your own page and its **personal link**, saved in this browser.
   Keep it: it opens every entry you make with it. Your page has
   **Competitions** (each competition you entered: tap one for a row per
   discipline, **Open this entry** to change or withdraw it, and **Enter
   another discipline**, under the same link) and **Your details** (your link,
   and your other links). On an entry, under **Judging and helping**, say what
   you can judge or help with. Once the timetable is published, your entry
   shows your flight and, under **Officiating**, when and where you judge or
   help. **My competition** gathers all your entries (each discipline),
   times, your timetable and calendar and the whole timetable on one page.

   Entered before, with a link for each entry? Each link still works and opens
   its entry on your page. Add the others under **Your details**, **Your other
   links**, so they're all on one page.
4. If the competition needs a coach's sign-off, send your coach the **Sign-off
   link** shown on your entry. They open it, check it and sign off with their
   name. If it approves coaches, first name your coach under **Your coach**,
   with their qualification and a photo or PDF of its certificate (up to 3 MB), and tap
   **Send to the organiser**. Once the organiser approves them, the link
   works and they sign off as themselves; your page shows the organiser's
   decision and any note. Changing your coach sends them to be approved
   again.

# 6. One link for all your roles

If you're a member who also coaches, your club's comp sec, entering some
competitions on your own, or helping run one, you can open all of it from
one link. On your page's **Your details** (a coach's is at the foot of their
page), under **Your other links**, paste another of your links and tap
**Add**. Then tabs at the top (Member, Coach, Competition secretary, On your own, or the
competition's) switch between them, whichever of your links you open. Each
link listed has **Undo**.

- Only add links that are yours. Pasting a link is how you show it's yours,
  so there's nothing to approve.
- A shared link, such as your club's comp sec link or a link the organiser
  gave a panel, opens from your page, but never opens your page: everyone
  else who has it sees only what it always showed. The comp sec or organiser
  sees who added it, and can remove it; replacing it ends it for everyone.
- If your comp sec makes you a coach too, there's no coach link: your member
  link opens your coach's page.
- If your comp sec gives you a **New link**, it opens only your pages at
  that club: add your other links to it again. The comp sec sees the links
  they make, so a link they make never opens your roles elsewhere.

# What's kept

Only names, clubs, levels and events, men or women where asked, synchro
partners, routines (with entries, and those members keep on their page),
video links, and the organiser's and coaches' notes, and
coaches' qualifications with their certificates where the club adds them. No
emails, birthdays or contact details. Videos are never uploaded: only their
links.

# Keeping your links safe

Competition entries are kept on the server. The links to them are saved in the
browser, so copy any you'd hate to lose: clearing the browser's data, or using
a private window, loses the saved links (not the entries). With all your roles
on one page, keeping that one link is enough.

Pages you've opened on your phone still open without a connection, as they were when last loaded, for up to 30 days. A page whose link has been replaced or deleted is dropped from the phone the next time it's opened online.

If a bar says **The app has been updated**, tap **Refresh**.
