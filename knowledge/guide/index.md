# Guide

For gymnasts, coaches, clubs and competition organisers using the app.

* [Features](features.md) - Everything the trampoline tariff calculator can do, in plain language for gymnasts, coaches, clubs and competition organisers.
* [User guide](user-guide.md) - Step-by-step instructions for gymnasts, coaches, clubs and competition organisers, covering how to add skills, build and check a routine, work on a competition level, write your own requirements, view, print and share routines, and run, enter and sign off competitions. Also as a [PDF](user-guide.pdf) to print or send on, built by `scripts/user-guide-pdf.py`.
